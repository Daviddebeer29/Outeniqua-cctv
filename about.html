Outeniqua CCTV & Off-site Monitoring was founded in 2016 with a clear purpose: bring enterprise-grade security technology to the homes and businesses of George and the Garden Route — at a price that makes sense for our community.

From our base at 91 York Street, Dormehls Drift, our team of trained security technicians and monitoring operators has grown to become one of the most trusted names in physical security across the region. We operate our own 24-hour Security Operations Centre (SOC), staffed around the clock by experienced operators who respond to events in real time.

Unlike national franchises, we are locally owned and deeply embedded in the Garden Route community. When you call us, you speak to someone in George — not a call centre in Johannesburg. When something happens, our response teams know the streets, the estates, and the people they protect.

We are proud to hold PSIRA registration and maintain full compliance with South Africa's POPIA privacy legislation, ensuring your data and footage are handled with the highest standards of security and discretion.

Our Values

Why Clients Choose Us

Three principles guide every installation, every monitoring shift, and every response we make.

Rapid Response

Our 24/7 SOC operators monitor your site in real time. When an event triggers, we react within seconds — dispatching a response team, contacting you, and notifying authorities as required. Our average on-screen response time is 60 seconds or less.

Local Expertise

Born and built in George, we know the Garden Route inside out. Our technicians understand the unique challenges of local properties — from coastal estates to industrial parks in the Outeniqua foothills. Local knowledge means better, faster security.

Full-Stack Security

From IP cameras and off-site monitoring to electric fencing, access control, boomgates, and solar backup — we design, install, and monitor complete security ecosystems. One company, one call, one point of accountability for your entire perimeter.
