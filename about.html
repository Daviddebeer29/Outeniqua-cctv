Who We Are

Outeniqua CCTV and Off-site Monitoring operate a nationwide network of approved CCTV installers, delivering professional installations through our approved network of top brands — including Dahua CCTV camera systems.

With deep technical expertise, we recommend the latest solutions tailored to your specific needs, all fully compatible with professional Off-Site Monitoring control rooms for seamless, around-the-clock oversight.

Our teams deliver installation services across all aspects of security and access control — including maintenance and Service Level Agreements for industrial, commercial, and domestic CCTV installations at sites throughout South Africa.

Our installers are accredited with 5 major finance facilities, offering fully customised rental solutions from 36 to 60 months to suit any business size or budget.
