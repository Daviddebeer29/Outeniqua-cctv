Our Story

Outeniqua CCTV was founded in George in 2016 by a team of security professionals frustrated by large national companies that treated clients as ticket numbers. We started with a simple promise: show up fast, install right, and keep watching.

Over the past decade we've grown from a two-person van to a full team of installation technicians, system engineers, and dedicated SOC operators — all based in the Western Cape. We cover George, Knysna, Mossel Bay, Oudtshoorn, and the broader Garden Route.

Our clients range from sole-trader retail stores to 300-unit residential estates, multi-site industrial complexes, and government facilities. Every job gets the same meticulous attention: site survey, tailored design, professional installation, and ongoing monitoring.

We've invested heavily in our control room infrastructure. Our SOC runs Milestone XProtect and Hikvision DeepinView analytics so our operators receive intelligent alerts — not noise — and can dispatch response within 60 seconds of a confirmed event.
