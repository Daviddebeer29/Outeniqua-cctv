Installation
This depends entirely on your property layout and threat model. A typical residential home needs 4–8 cameras to cover all entry points and blind spots. A retail store or warehouse could need 8–20+. Our site inspection determines the exact number — we never oversell cameras you don't need.
For permanent installations we always recommend PoE (Power over Ethernet) wired cameras — more reliable, better image quality, and no Wi-Fi dependence. For locations where cabling isn't practical we use 4G cellular cameras. We never recommend consumer Wi-Fi cameras for security purposes.
A standard 8-camera residential installation takes 4–6 hours. A commercial system with 16–24 cameras typically takes 1–2 days. We clean up after ourselves and won't leave your property until the system is fully tested and you've been shown how to use the app.
Pricing & Contracts
Every installation is priced individually. A basic 4-camera residential system typically starts from R8,000–R12,000 fully installed. Monitoring fees depend on the number of cameras and response package. We provide itemised quotes with no hidden fees — what you see is what you pay.
Our monitoring agreements are typically 12-month contracts, with month-to-month available at a slight premium. We believe in earning your business every month rather than locking you in — our renewal rate speaks for itself. Hardware and installation are billed separately and are yours to keep regardless.
All hardware carries the manufacturer's warranty (typically 2–3 years). Our workmanship carries a 12-month labour warranty — if anything we installed fails due to our workmanship, we fix it at no charge. Hikvision equipment is covered under their South African warranty programme.
Technical
We primarily install Hikvision and Dahua — the two most reliable and widely supported brands for commercial and residential CCTV. Both offer excellent AI analytics, mobile app access, and long-term spare parts availability. We are a certified Hikvision installer with direct warranty support.
Storage duration depends on the number of cameras, resolution, and NVR hard drive size. A typical system stores 30 days of continuous HD footage. We can configure systems for 7, 14, 30, or 60-day retention depending on your requirements. For POPIA compliance, we typically recommend 30-day retention for business premises.
