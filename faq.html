Monitoring

Off-site monitoring means your CCTV cameras are watched 24/7 by trained operators at a remote Security Operations Centre (SOC) — rather than relying on passive recording alone. When a camera detects suspicious activity, a live operator visually verifies the event and immediately dispatches armed response if needed.

This is fundamentally different from a traditional alarm system, which relies on automated detection and often generates false call-outs. With visual verification, every genuine incident is acted on within minutes — at any hour, any day of the year. Outeniqua CCTV's SOC operates 365 days a year, including public holidays.

Load Shedding

Standard CCTV cameras will stop working during load shedding unless the system has battery or solar backup. A camera that goes offline during a power cut is a direct security gap — and load shedding follows a predictable schedule that criminals exploit.

Outeniqua CCTV installs integrated solar panel and lithium battery solutions specifically sized for CCTV systems, providing a minimum of 12 hours of backup power through any stage of load shedding. Your cameras stay live, recording, and connected to the monitoring centre even when the grid is down.

Compliance & Legal

Yes. Under the Private Security Industry Regulation Act (PSIRA), CCTV installation and monitoring services must be performed by PSIRA-registered businesses and technicians. This is not optional — operating without registration is a criminal offence under the Act.

Using an unregistered installer also has practical consequences: your insurance provider may reject security-related claims if the system was not installed by a registered company. Outeniqua CCTV is fully PSIRA-registered, and all installations comply with the relevant SANS safety standards.

CCTV vs Alarm

A traditional alarm system detects an intrusion event and triggers an alert — it cannot tell you what happened or whether the threat is real. A CCTV system provides continuous visual surveillance and stores recorded footage, but without someone watching, incidents may go unnoticed until after the fact.

Off-site CCTV monitoring combines both: your cameras provide live footage 24/7, and trained operators visually verify every alarm event before dispatching armed response. This eliminates false call-outs, guarantees a response to genuine threats, and provides video evidence for every incident — something a standalone alarm system cannot offer.

Electric Fencing

Yes, electric fencing is legal in South Africa for residential and commercial properties — provided it is installed correctly and in compliance with SANS 10222-3 safety standards. This standard governs maximum output voltage, mandatory warning signage, earthing requirements, and energiser specifications.

Non-compliant electric fencing can result in fines, insurance rejection, and legal liability if someone is injured. Outeniqua CCTV installs PSIRA-approved electric fencing systems that are fully SANS 10222-3 compliant, and we issue a compliance certificate upon completion of every installation.
