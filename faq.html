Monitoring

What is off-site monitoring?

Off-site (or remote) monitoring means your CCTV cameras stream live video to our Security Operations Centre (SOC) in George, where trained operators watch your property around the clock. Unlike recordings that you review after the fact, live monitoring allows us to detect and respond to threats as they happen — before damage or loss occurs.

How does the 24/7 SOC work?

Our SOC operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Operators monitor multiple camera feeds simultaneously using advanced video management software with AI-assisted motion detection and analytics. When an anomaly is detected — whether motion in a restricted area, a fence breach, or an alarm trigger — the operator assesses the live feed and takes immediate action.

Action may include: contacting you directly, dispatching an armed response team, alerting SAPS, or activating site lighting and audio deterrents depending on the agreed response protocol for your property.

What happens when an alarm triggers?

When an alarm or motion event triggers, a SOC operator immediately pulls up the live camera feed for your property to visually verify the event — this eliminates false alarm callouts and wasted response team deployments. If the threat is confirmed:

1. You are contacted immediately via phone or WhatsApp.
2. An armed response team is dispatched (via our response partners).
3. SAPS may be notified if required.
4. A full incident report is logged and sent to you.

Our average on-screen operator response time is 60 seconds from trigger to active assessment.

Installation

How long does installation take?

Installation time depends on the size and complexity of your property. A standard residential installation of 4–8 cameras typically takes one full day. Larger commercial or estate installations may take 2–5 days. We provide a detailed installation timeline as part of your quote, and we work neatly and efficiently to minimise disruption to your property.

Do you supply the equipment, or do I need to buy my own?

We supply all equipment as part of our service. We source professional-grade cameras, NVRs/DVRs, cabling, power units, and mounting hardware — we do not install client-supplied consumer equipment, as we cannot guarantee its performance or compatibility. All equipment comes with a manufacturer warranty and our own installation guarantee.

What camera brands do you use?

We work with leading professional security brands including Hikvision, Dahua, and Uniview — all industry-standard brands used by commercial and government security installations worldwide. We select the appropriate camera specification (resolution, IR range, weatherproofing, lens type) based on your site's specific requirements rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Technical

Can I view my cameras remotely on my phone?

Yes. All our installations include remote viewing setup via a dedicated mobile app (such as iVMS-4500 or Hik-Connect for Hikvision systems). You can view live and recorded footage from anywhere in the world on your smartphone or tablet, provided you have a data or Wi-Fi connection. We configure this during installation and provide a walkthrough so you're comfortable using it.

What happens to my cameras during load-shedding?

By default, cameras and NVRs will lose power during load-shedding unless backup power is in place. We strongly recommend — and can supply and install — UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) units and/or solar battery backup systems specifically designed for CCTV infrastructure. This keeps your cameras and recorder running through Eskom outages. Ask us about our solar backup solutions when you enquire.

Do you offer solar backup for CCTV?

Absolutely. We offer purpose-built solar and lithium battery backup systems for CCTV installations — ensuring uninterrupted operation regardless of load-shedding stage. Our solar solutions are sized specifically for your camera count and consumption, and can be added to both new installations and existing systems. Contact us to discuss your requirements.
