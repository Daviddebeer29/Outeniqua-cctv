Privacy Policy & POPIA Compliance

Last updated May 2026

Outeniqua CCTV & Off-site Monitoring ("Outeniqua CCTV", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) and all applicable South African privacy legislation. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal information.

1. Who We Are

Outeniqua CCTV & Off-site Monitoring is a private security services company registered and operating in George, Western Cape, South Africa. We provide CCTV installation, off-site monitoring, access control, electric fencing, and related security services.

Registered address: 91 York Street, Dormehls Drift, George, Western Cape, 6529.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity information: full name, ID number (where required for PSIRA compliance), and business registration details for commercial clients.
  • Contact information: telephone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses.
  • Property information: site addresses, property layouts, and security vulnerability assessments.
  • CCTV footage: video recordings captured by cameras we install and/or monitor on your behalf. This is processed under a separate CCTV-specific processing agreement.
  • Payment information: billing details and transaction records (we do not store card details; payments are processed via secure third-party payment processors).
  • Communication records: emails, WhatsApp messages, and call logs where relevant to service delivery or dispute resolution.
  • Website usage data: anonymous usage analytics via cookies (see Section 9).

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information for the following lawful purposes:

  • To provide, manage, and maintain security services you have contracted us for.
  • To respond to enquiries, quotation requests, and support tickets.
  • To process payments and maintain financial records as required by law.
  • To comply with PSIRA registration requirements and South African security industry regulations.
  • To send service-related communications (maintenance notices, contract renewals, urgent alerts).
  • To improve our services and website based on anonymous usage analytics.
  • To protect the safety and security of our clients, staff, and third parties.

We do not use your personal information for unsolicited marketing without your prior consent.

4. CCTV Footage — Special Provisions

CCTV footage captured at client premises is processed under specific provisions:

  • Footage is captured solely for the purpose of security monitoring, incident investigation, and evidence preservation.
  • All footage is stored securely with access restricted to authorised SOC operators and relevant client personnel.
  • Footage is typically retained for 30 days before overwriting, unless a specific incident requires preservation for longer.
  • Footage will only be shared with law enforcement, insurance companies, or legal representatives when legally required or with client consent.
  • Visible CCTV notices are required at all monitored premises as required by POPIA and the PSIRA Act.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Armed response companies: limited contact and location details shared when responding to a confirmed alarm activation.
  • PSIRA: registration and compliance information as required by the Private Security Industry Regulation Authority.
  • Service providers: trusted third parties who assist in operating our business (e.g., cloud storage providers, VMS software vendors) under data processing agreements.
  • Law enforcement: when legally compelled by court order or statutory authority.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • Encrypted storage for all client data and CCTV recordings.
  • Role-based access controls — only authorised staff can access client information.
  • Regular security audits of our SOC infrastructure and data systems.
  • Staff training on data protection and privacy obligations.

In the event of a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected data subjects as required by POPIA within the statutory timeframe.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject you have the following rights under POPIA:

  • Right of access: you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: you may request that inaccurate or incomplete information be corrected.
  • Right to deletion: you may request deletion of your personal information where we no longer have a legal basis to retain it.
  • Right to object: you may object to the processing of your personal information for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to complain: you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Information Officer using the details below.
