Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 May 2026

Outeniqua CCTV & Off-site Monitoring ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or contact form on this website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • WhatsApp number (if provided)
  • Property address or location (if provided)
  • Service enquiry details

We also collect anonymous usage data through cookies (see Cookie Policy below).

2. Why We Collect Your Information

We use your personal information solely to:

  • Respond to your service enquiry or quote request
  • Schedule site inspections and assessments
  • Communicate with you about our services
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. How We Store Your Information

Enquiry data submitted through this website is stored securely. We retain contact information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us directly.

4. Your Rights Under POPIA

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of your rights, contact our Information Officer at: info@outeniquacctv.co.za or call 060 636 2700.

PSIRA Registration

Effective date: 15 May 2026

Outeniqua CCTV & Off-site Monitoring operates in compliance with the Private Security Industry Regulatory Authority (PSIRA) requirements under the Private Security Industry Regulation Act, 56 of 2001.

Our installations and monitoring services are carried out by PSIRA-approved personnel. All electric fencing systems are installed in compliance with SANS 10222-3 safety standards.

For verification of our PSIRA registration status or to report a compliance concern, you may contact PSIRA directly at psira.co.za or on 012 337 0000.

Cookie Policy

Effective date: 15 May 2026

This website uses cookies to improve your browsing experience and to help us understand how visitors use our site. A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by your web browser.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: Required for the website to function correctly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g. which pages are visited most). We use anonymised, aggregated data only.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your cookie consent choice so you are not asked again on every visit.

We do not use advertising, tracking, or third-party marketing cookies. We do not share cookie data with advertisers.

Managing Cookies

You can decline non-essential cookies using the cookie banner when you first visit our site. You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features.
