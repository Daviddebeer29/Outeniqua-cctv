1. Introduction

Outeniqua CCTV & Off-site Monitoring ("we", "our", or "the Company") is a private security company registered and operating in George, Western Cape, South Africa. We are committed to protecting the privacy and personal information of our clients, website visitors, and all individuals whose information comes into our possession in the course of our operations.

This Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, use, store, share, and protect personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, No. 4 of 2013 (POPIA), which came into full effect on 1 July 2021.

By using our services, visiting our website, or providing us with your personal information, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

In the course of providing our security services, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity information: Full name, identity number (where required for security clearance purposes).
  • Contact details: Telephone numbers, WhatsApp numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses.
  • Property information: Site layout, access details, and security specifications relating to your property.
  • CCTV footage: Video recordings captured by camera systems we install and/or monitor on your behalf.
  • Communication records: Records of phone calls, emails, and WhatsApp messages for service delivery and quality assurance purposes.
  • Billing information: Banking details and payment records for invoicing and debit order purposes.
  • Website usage data: IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and referring URLs collected via standard web server logs and analytics tools.

We collect personal information only for lawful, specific, and clearly defined purposes. We do not collect information that is excessive or irrelevant to the provision of our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery: To install, configure, and maintain your CCTV and security systems.
  • Off-site monitoring: To operate our 24/7 Security Operations Centre (SOC) and monitor your property on your behalf.
  • Incident response: To contact you, emergency contacts, or response partners in the event of a security incident.
  • Billing and administration: To generate invoices, process payments, and maintain accounting records.
  • Client communication: To provide service updates, respond to enquiries, and send relevant operational information.
  • Legal compliance: To comply with applicable South African legislation, including POPIA, the Private Security Industry Regulation Act (PSIRA), and the Occupational Health and Safety Act.
  • Service improvement: To analyse service performance and improve our systems and operations.

We will never use your personal information for unsolicited marketing purposes without your explicit consent, and we will never sell your personal information to any third party.

4. CCTV Footage

CCTV footage recorded on systems we install on your property is your property. Where we provide off-site monitoring, footage is streamed to and stored on our secure systems for monitoring purposes only.

Standard retention period: CCTV footage recorded on client NVR/DVR systems is typically retained for 30 days on a rolling basis before being overwritten, unless a longer retention period is specifically requested or required for an ongoing investigation.

Footage stored in our SOC systems for monitoring purposes is retained for 7 days as a standard unless flagged as evidence for an incident. Incident footage is retained for a minimum of 90 days or until the matter is resolved, whichever is longer.

Access to footage: You may request access to footage of your property at any time. Requests from third parties (including law enforcement) will be handled strictly in accordance with applicable legislation. We will not provide footage to any party without your consent, except where required by court order or by SAPS in the course of a criminal investigation.

CCTV placement notices: Where we install cameras in shared or semi-public areas (such as estate entrances or common areas), we ensure that appropriate CCTV notification signage is displayed in compliance with POPIA and the relevant codes of practice.

5. Data Sharing

We may share your personal information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Armed response partners: Operational information necessary for dispatch and response to security incidents at your property.
  • Law enforcement: Where required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of individuals.
  • Service providers: Third-party suppliers and contractors who assist us in delivering our services (e.g., equipment suppliers, IT support), subject to confidentiality agreements.
  • Emergency contacts: Individuals you have designated to be contacted in the event of a security incident at your property.

We do not share personal information with any other parties without your prior written consent. All third parties who receive personal information from us are required to comply with POPIA and to maintain appropriate data security measures.

6. Data Security

We implement and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encrypted storage and transmission of sensitive data.
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel only.
  • Regular security assessments of our IT infrastructure.
  • Physical security measures at our office and SOC facility.
  • Staff training on data privacy and security obligations.

In the event of a security compromise or data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator within the timeframes required by POPIA.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information held by us:

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to correction: You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Information Officer using the details in Section 8 below. We will respond to all requests within 30 days of receipt.

The Information Regulator of South Africa can be contacted at: inforeg@justice.gov.za or by visiting www.inforegulator.org.za.
