Eight Ways We Protect You

Every solution is designed, installed, and supported by our local team in George — one company, total accountability.

CCTV Camera Installation

Professional IP and HD-analogue camera systems for homes, estates, offices, and industrial facilities. We assess your site, design the optimal camera layout, and handle full installation and cabling — ensuring every blind spot is covered and every angle is sharp, day and night.

24-Hour Off-site Monitoring

Our Security Operations Centre (SOC) watches your cameras around the clock, 365 days a year. Trained operators respond to live events within 60 seconds — verifying threats, dispatching armed response, and alerting you immediately. Far more reliable than motion-triggered emails or self-monitoring apps.

Control Room Design & Setup

Need an on-site command centre? We design and build custom control rooms for estates, industrial complexes, and large commercial sites. From NVR/DVR rack infrastructure and multi-screen video walls to network switching, UPS power, and operator workstations — fully integrated and ready to run.

Solar Power for CCTV

Load-shedding is a real threat to security. We install purpose-built solar and battery backup systems for CCTV infrastructure, ensuring your cameras and monitoring remain fully operational during Eskom outages — day or night, regardless of loadshedding stage.

Get a Quote

ClearVu High-Security Fencing

ClearVu is the benchmark in anti-climb, anti-cut perimeter fencing. Woven from heavy-gauge steel wire, it is extremely difficult to scale or penetrate while remaining virtually transparent — maintaining visibility and aesthetics. Ideal for estates, schools, and industrial boundaries.

Access Control

Control who enters your premises with precision. We install keypad, proximity card, biometric fingerprint, and facial recognition access systems — integrated with your CCTV for complete entry logging. Suitable for single doors, multi-gate estates, and complex multi-zone commercial environments.

Get a Quote

Boomgate Systems

Automated boomgates for estate entrances, parking facilities, and commercial driveways. We supply and install heavy-duty, all-weather boom barriers with remote control, keypad, or ANPR (licence plate recognition) activation — integrated with your intercom and access control for seamless vehicle management.

Get a Quote

Electric Fencing

A proven first line of defence for residential and commercial properties. Our electric fence installations comply fully with South African legislation (OHS Act and SANS 10222) and are coupled with monitored energisers — so when a fence is tampered with or breached, our SOC is alerted instantly.
