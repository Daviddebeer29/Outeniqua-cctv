02 Monitoring

What is off-site CCTV monitoring?

A camera is only as effective as the person watching it. Our state-of-the-art Security Operations Centre (SOC) provides true 24/7 live monitoring — not just recording — so that the moment something happens on your property, a trained operator is already responding.

Unlike traditional alarm systems that rely on auto-detection alone, our operators verify each event visually before dispatching armed response. This dramatically reduces false call-outs and guarantees that every genuine incident is acted on immediately — at any hour of the day or night.

Our monitoring platform supports residential estates, industrial sites, retail stores, schools, and commercial premises of any scale. Monthly monitoring packages are available with no lock-in contracts.

04 Energy

Solar Power for CCTV

Load-shedding is South Africa's most predictable security vulnerability — when the power goes off, so do cameras that aren't backed up. Our integrated solar power solutions ensure your CCTV system stays live and recording through every stage of load-shedding, power failures, and grid outages.

We design and install solar panel arrays, lithium battery storage systems, and pure-sine inverters specifically sized for CCTV and monitoring applications. Systems are engineered for minimum 12-hour battery autonomy under full camera load, with solar recharging maintaining indefinite off-grid capability in good conditions.

Whether retrofitting an existing system or integrating solar from the start of a new installation, we deliver a seamless power solution that removes load-shedding as a security risk — permanently.

05 Perimeter

ClearVu High-Security Fencing

Extremely strong and designed to prevent climbing or cutting

ClearVu is South Africa's leading high-security perimeter fencing system — engineered specifically for environments where intrusion prevention matters as much as detection. The welded mesh panel design is virtually unclimbable and highly resistant to cutting, making it the preferred perimeter solution for schools, industrial facilities, residential estates, and government installations.

Unlike traditional palisade or clearview fencing, ClearVu's rigid panel structure is extremely difficult to deform, penetrate, or scale — acting as a genuine physical barrier rather than just a boundary marker. The open mesh design also maintains clear sightlines for camera systems, meaning your CCTV cameras work with the fencing rather than around it.

We supply, deliver, and install ClearVu fencing across all heights and configurations — including anti-dig bases, razor-top additions, and integration with electric fencing and beam detection systems.

07 Vehicle

Boomgate Systems

Security Boom — Controlled Vehicle Access

Vehicle access is one of the highest-risk entry points for any secured property. Our automated boomgate systems create a controlled vehicle lane that integrates seamlessly with your access control, CCTV, and intercom infrastructure — giving you complete command over who drives in and out.

We supply and install industrial and commercial-grade boomgate barriers built with aluminium alloy bodies, rated dustproof and waterproof to IP66 with an IK10 impact rating — designed to withstand South Africa's outdoor conditions day after day without compromise.

All boomgate installations include battery backup to ensure operation during load-shedding, remote management capability, and a safety obstruction sensor to prevent barrier strikes on vehicles or pedestrians.

ANPR integration recognises licence plates in just 0.8 seconds — enabling accurate, efficient vehicle access for pre-authorised vehicles without requiring them to stop, while automatically flagging unregistered plates for operator review.

08 Perimeter

Is electric fencing right for your property?

Electric fencing remains one of the most effective and visible deterrents against perimeter breach. The combination of a physical barrier and an active energised warning creates a strong psychological and practical deterrent that discourages most intrusion attempts before they even begin.

We supply, install, and maintain electric fencing systems for residential homes, commercial properties, industrial sites, and agricultural holdings across the Garden Route. All energiser systems comply with SANS 10222-3 safety standards and are PSIRA-approved — ensuring your system is both effective and legally compliant.

Our after-installation service includes regular compliance checks, fault-finding, strand replacements, energiser servicing, and alarm notification integration — so your perimeter remains active and alarm-linked at all times.
